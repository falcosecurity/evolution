# Maintainers

These lists are generated from the accepted [`.project` metadata](https://github.com/falcosecurity/.project).
Maintainers are approvers in root or nested `OWNERS` files of active repositories.
Core maintainers are root approvers of repositories classified as `Core`.
Reviewers and emeritus owners do not acquire maintainer status through those roles.

Change roles in the relevant `OWNERS` file. Edit names and companies in
[config/affiliations.yaml](https://github.com/falcosecurity/.project/blob/main/config/affiliations.yaml).
See [metadata synchronization](METADATA.md) for the review and publication flow.
